The story behind Ka Mashila

A family legacy, one tray at a time

Ka Mashila Poultry Farm was founded in Nkuzana Village with a simple mission: to provide fresh quality eggs and delicious atchar to our community.

The man in our logo is the founder’s late grandfather. Although he passed away before the founder was born, his legacy continues to inspire every day. He sits at the heart of the brand so that his name and memory can live on through something meaningful that serves the community.

Every tray of eggs and every bucket of atchar sold by Ka Mashila Poultry Farm represents hard work, family values, and the belief that our roots should never be forgotten.

Thank you to everyone who supports this journey. Your support helps us grow while keeping a family legacy alive.
